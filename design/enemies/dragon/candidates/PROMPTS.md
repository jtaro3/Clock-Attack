## 01_ember

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: ONE standalone enemy dragon sprite for a medieval fantasy pixel-art action game, intended final transparent PNG 94x94 pixels. Render as an enlarged version of a deliberate 94x94 logical pixel grid. True pixel art with crisp square pixel clusters and a restrained cohesive palette, no painted gradients, no blur, no vector curves. Style fits small colorful round slime enemies, a blue-and-gold armored pixel knight, green grassy tiles and wooden crates. Dragon is BIPEDAL, standing upright on exactly two clearly visible sturdy hind legs, with two short separate front arms. Two clearly visible bat-like wings grow from its back, spread slightly to the sides, both must fit completely inside canvas. Horned dragon head with long snout and TWO ivory FANGS visibly protruding from the corners of its mouth even while mouth is mostly closed. Reptilian creature, not a humanoid knight; no clothing, no weapon. Full body including head, feet, wing tips and tail, all centered with generous transparent margins, three-quarter front view facing slightly to viewer's left. Static idle standing pose, single frame. Strong dark outline, clean readable silhouette, upper-left highlights and lower-right shadow in discrete pixel colors. Clearly a dragon with scales, clawed toes, horned head, visible teeth and folded curved tail. Approachable game stylization but formidable enemy, not baby chibi. No animation frames, no multiple characters, no scene, no platform, no shadow outside creature, no glow, no text, no border, no checkerboard. Genuinely transparent background. Variant 1 EMBER: classic red dragon, red-orange scales, pale cream belly, ochre horns, warm orange inner wings. Compact balanced medium build, two swept-back horns, confident fierce expression.
